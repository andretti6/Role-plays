Group Project

The Future of Work

Design a job where people are happy, grow, and everyone wins.

Start the mission ↓ Useful English ↓
1

Your Mission

Work in groups. You are the designers of the future of work.

  1. 1Choose or invent a type of work. It can be real, new, or from the future.
  2. 2Decide how people work: hours, place, team, tools, rights, and responsibilities.
  3. 3Plan happiness and growth for every worker.
  4. 4Make it a win-win: the company, the workers, and the customers all win.
⚠ Everyone must win. No one can lose.
2

Design Board

This is only a suggestion. Talk about these questions in your group. You can change them or add your own ideas.

Our Work

  • What do we do?
  • What do we make or sell?
  • Who are our customers?

How We Work

  • What hours do we work?
  • Where do we work?
  • How big is the team?
  • What tools do we use?

Rights

  • What must every worker always have?
  • Think about pay, safety, time, and respect.

Responsibilities

  • What must every worker always do?
  • Think about quality, teamwork, and honesty.

Happiness & Growth

  • What makes people happy at work?
  • How do people learn and grow?

Win-Win

  • 🏢 How does the company win?
  • 👷 How do the workers win?
  • 🛒 How do the customers win?
