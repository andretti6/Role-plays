Real World Example

🌍 Real Story

Europe Fined Google €403 Million

In 2026, the European Union fined Google €403 million because the company used people's location data without clear permission. Under European law (called the GDPR), companies must be honest about what data they collect and why.

The GDPR says that personal data must be processed in a lawful, fair, and transparent way. Google did not follow these rules, so the European Union took action.

💡 Why this matters: This shows that privacy laws can work in real life. When companies break the rules, they must pay. The European Union's GDPR is now a model for many other countries, including Brazil, Japan, and South Korea.
